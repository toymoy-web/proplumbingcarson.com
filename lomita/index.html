Plumbing in Lomita, Near Carson

Lomita is one of the smaller cities in the South Bay, covered entirely by a single ZIP code, 90717, and bordered by Torrance, Harbor City, and Rolling Hills Estates, with Carson close by to the east. It's a primarily residential community, and its best-known landmark is probably the Lomita Railroad Museum, a small museum modeled after a 1900s-era train depot that draws visitors from well beyond the city's own small footprint.

Being a compact, mostly built-out city, Lomita's housing stock skews toward the same postwar construction period common throughout this part of the South Bay, which means original galvanized supply lines and cast-iron drain systems are still common in a meaningful share of homes here. Because the city is small and doesn't have a large commercial or industrial base of its own, most of our calls in Lomita are residential.

Lomita's compact size also means most addresses in the city are within a short drive of our other South Bay coverage areas, so a call here doesn't typically mean a long wait compared to a more spread-out community.

Lomita was incorporated in 1964 and has stayed almost entirely residential since, without developing much of an independent commercial or industrial base of its own, which is part of why the city relies on neighboring Torrance and Harbor City for most non-residential services.

That same lack of heavy industry, however, also means less of the ground disturbance and utility congestion that can complicate underground plumbing work in more industrial parts of the South Bay. Lots in Lomita also tend to run smaller and more uniform than in some neighboring cities, which generally makes locating shutoffs and cleanouts more predictable from one property to the next.

Since Lomita is a single-ZIP city, the water heater installation and slab leak calls we get nearby usually come from the bordering 90501 ZIP in Torrance or the 90710 area of Harbor City, both within a few minutes of Lomita itself.
