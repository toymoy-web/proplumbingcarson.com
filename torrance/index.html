Serving Torrance, CA

Plumbing in Torrance, Near Carson

The 90502 ZIP code covers the West Carson community and the eastern edge of Torrance, right beside the Harbor Freeway and Carson's western boundary. Many homes here carry a Torrance mailing address but sit in unincorporated West Carson, which can change which agency handles permits, so we confirm the jurisdiction when we schedule permitted work.

West Carson has about 22,900 residents and includes Harbor-UCLA Medical Center on West Carson Street. The mix of single-family streets, apartment buildings, and medical and light commercial properties means we handle residential repairs and commercial plumbing calls, including restroom fixture repair and backflow testing, on the same route.

Torrance's wider housing stock includes plenty of homes from the postwar decades, and the homes in and around 90502 follow the same pattern as other older South Bay properties. Original supply lines and drain systems are now decades past their installation date in a meaningful share of cases, so we ask about a home's age before we arrive.

Torrance has a long manufacturing and aerospace history, and Torrance Municipal Airport at 3301 Airport Drive, in the 90505 ZIP on the city's southwest side, is one of its best-known landmarks. Robinson Helicopter has its headquarters there. That industrial legacy still shapes commercial plumbing work across the city, from larger fixture counts to backflow assemblies sized for commercial use.

We also take on faucet repair and water softener installation calls from the neighboring 90504 ZIP and the 90247 area of Gardena, both a short drive from our regular Torrance coverage.
