Common Faucet & Sink Issues

💧

Constant Dripping

Usually a worn cartridge, washer, or O-ring inside the faucet body that's no longer sealing properly.

🚰

Low Water Pressure at the Faucet

Often mineral buildup inside the aerator or, less commonly, a supply line restriction.

🌊

Leak Under the Sink

A loose supply line connection, worn P-trap, or failed shutoff valve can all leak in slightly different ways.

🔧

Faucet Handle Loose or Hard to Turn

Internal wear on the cartridge or valve stem, which usually gets worse rather than better on its own.

In Carson, CA

Faucet and Sink Repair in Carson Homes

Faucet repair is usually one of the more straightforward calls we handle: most drips and handle problems come down to a worn cartridge, washer, or O-ring, and identifying which faucet brand and cartridge type is installed lets us complete most repairs in a single visit rather than needing to special-order a part.

Under-sink leaks are worth taking seriously even when they seem minor, since a slow leak inside a cabinet can go unnoticed for weeks while it damages the cabinet base and, in some cases, the flooring underneath. We check supply line connections, the P-trap, and the shutoff valves whenever we're called for an under-sink leak, since addressing only the obvious drip sometimes misses a second, smaller leak nearby.

For homes with Carson's moderately hard water, mineral buildup inside faucet aerators and cartridges happens faster than in a soft-water area, which is a common reason a faucet that was recently repaired starts losing pressure again within a year or two.

Bathroom sink faucets tend to fail differently than kitchen faucets, since they're used more frequently but under lighter daily demand, and often develop a slow drip at the spout rather than a full connection failure. We stock common bathroom faucet cartridges alongside kitchen parts, since a bathroom with two or three sinks sometimes needs more than one repair scheduled at the same visit.

Pricing Transparency

What Affects the Price of Faucet & Sink Repair

We can't give an accurate number without seeing the job, but these are the real factors that move the price up or down.

Faucet brand and part availability

A common cartridge is stocked and installed same-visit; an uncommon or discontinued brand sometimes needs a special-order part.

Shutoff valve condition

Old, corroded shutoff valves are often replaced at the same time as the faucet repair to avoid a future separate call.

Under-sink plumbing condition

Additional wear found under the sink during the repair, like a failing P-trap, can add to the original scope.

On insurance: Faucet and fixture wear is routine maintenance in the eyes of nearly every policy, so this generally isn't insurance territory. Pricing is confirmed once we see the actual condition.
