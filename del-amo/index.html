About Del Amo

The Del Amo Area of Carson

Del Amo is one of the larger residential areas of Carson, about two square miles by the measure of mapping sites. East Del Amo Boulevard runs through it, and East University Drive, East Turmont Street and East Gladwick Street are among its longer streets. Central Avenue and Avalon Boulevard are listed as major roads nearby. Homes here use ZIP codes 90745 and 90746.

Census-based estimates on the city-data site count about 15,000 residents and show nearly all housing units as detached houses. The same estimates place roughly half of the homes in the 1960s, the largest share of any decade. These are estimates, not a survey of your block, but they describe a neighborhood of single-family houses rather than apartment buildings.

One naming note helps with routing. The Del Amo Fashion Center and the Del Amo area around it are in Torrance, which is a different city. This page covers the Carson neighborhood. If your address is in Torrance, see our Torrance page instead.

Practical Guidance

Plumbing for Single-Family Homes in Del Amo

In a house that has stood for decades, the most useful first step is information. Ask for a camera inspection of the sewer line before a remodel, check the water pressure at an outdoor spigot, and have the age and condition of the water heater looked at before it fails. We identify what is installed on site instead of assuming.

One slow sink is usually a local clog. When several fixtures drain slowly together, or a toilet gurgles when the washer runs, the problem is more likely in the main line, and drain cleaning with a camera look is the right start. If the camera shows damage, we explain the sewer line repair options before any work begins.

A running toilet is often a quick fix with a new flapper or fill valve. If the tank or bowl is cracked, replacement makes more sense. Moving a fixture or changing drain layout in a remodel can need a permit, so call the City of Carson Building and Safety Division first.
