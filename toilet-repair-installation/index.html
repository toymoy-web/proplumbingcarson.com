Toilet Repair and Replacement in Carson

Most toilet repairs are straightforward: a new flapper, fill valve, or wax ring resolves the majority of running, leaking, or clogging complaints without needing to replace the whole fixture. We diagnose the actual failing part rather than replacing the entire internal mechanism when a single component would do.

Replacement makes more sense when a toilet is significantly older, particularly pre-1994 models that use much more water per flush than current California-mandated low-flow standards, or when the porcelain itself is cracked. For homeowners looking to cut water use, newer dual-flush and high-efficiency models can meaningfully reduce water bills over time compared to an older unit.

Installation includes checking that the floor flange is in good condition and properly leveled, since a failing flange is a common reason a newly installed toilet still develops a base leak within a year or two if it's not addressed at install time.

For homes with more than one bathroom, it's worth mentioning if other toilets are showing early signs of the same issue, since fixtures installed around the same time often wear out on a similar timeline. Addressing a second toilet's flapper or fill valve during the same visit is usually more efficient than scheduling a separate call a few months later.
