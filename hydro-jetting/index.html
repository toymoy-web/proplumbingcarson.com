Not Sure What's Wrong?

Snaking Keeps Working, But the Clog Comes Right Back?

A standard drain snake punches through a blockage and creates a path for water to flow again, but it doesn't necessarily remove buildup coating the walls of the pipe along its length. If a drain clogs again within weeks of being snaked, repeatedly, that pattern is the clearest sign that the pipe needs a more thorough cleaning than snaking alone provides.

Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the full interior surface of a pipe, removing grease, scale, sludge, and even minor root intrusion, rather than just clearing a path through the middle. It's a more thorough fix for a chronic problem, though it's not always the right first step for a simple, isolated clog.

In Carson, CA

Hydro Jetting for Carson's Older Pipe Systems

Carson's older cast-iron and clay drain and sewer lines are particularly prone to the kind of internal buildup that hydro jetting addresses, since decades of use leave scale, grease, and sediment coating the pipe's interior surface, gradually narrowing the effective diameter even when the pipe isn't fully blocked.

We always run a camera inspection before jetting a line we haven't already assessed, since jetting a severely compromised or fractured pipe at full pressure can worsen existing damage rather than help it. For a structurally sound pipe with buildup rather than a break, jetting is a safe and thorough way to restore the line closer to its original flow capacity.

For commercial kitchens in Carson, periodic hydro jetting of grease-prone lines is often more cost-effective as a scheduled maintenance step than waiting for a full backup to force an emergency call.
