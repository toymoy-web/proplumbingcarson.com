Getting in Touch

How to Reach Us

The fastest way to reach us for anything urgent, a burst pipe, a sewage backup, no hot water, is a phone call. A technician or dispatcher answers around the clock, and for a true emergency you'll get a same-day or immediate response depending on the situation. For non-emergency questions, like scheduling a water heater installation or asking about a specific repair, email works well too, though a phone call still tends to get you a faster answer since we can ask a few quick questions about the job.

We service Carson directly, along with Long Beach, Torrance, Compton, Wilmington, and Lomita. If you're just outside these areas, call anyway, coverage can often extend further depending on the job and your specific address.
