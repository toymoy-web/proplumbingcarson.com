Shower & Tub Installation in Carson Homes

New shower and tub installation involves more than setting the fixture in place. Proper waterproofing behind the surround, correctly sloped drain connections, and code-compliant water supply lines all matter more to long-term performance than the visible fixture itself. A beautiful new shower installed with a poor waterproofing membrane will develop hidden water damage within a few years regardless of how it looks.

Tub-to-shower conversions are a common request in Carson, particularly for homeowners looking to age in place or improve accessibility. This kind of conversion typically involves modifying the drain location and sometimes the surrounding wall structure, which is more involved than a straightforward like-for-like replacement.

For older Carson homes with original 1950s and 1960s-era bathroom plumbing, a new shower or tub installation is often a good opportunity to also update the supply lines and shutoff valves behind the wall, since accessing that area for the fixture work makes future access much harder once everything's closed back up.

Glass shower enclosures and surround panels have become a common upgrade during installation, replacing older shower curtain setups or dated fiberglass surrounds. We coordinate the plumbing rough-in to match whatever finish materials you're installing, since the drain and valve placement needs to line up precisely with the enclosure or surround before tile or panel work begins.
