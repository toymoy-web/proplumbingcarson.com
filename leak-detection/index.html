Not Sure What's Wrong?

High Water Bill With No Obvious Cause?

An unexplained jump in your water bill, especially if no fixtures are visibly running or dripping, is one of the most common signs of a hidden leak, often in a slab, a wall cavity, or an underground supply line between the meter and the house. A damp patch on flooring, a warm spot on a slab floor, or the water meter still spinning when everything in the house is off are other common clues.

The goal of leak detection is to locate the exact source before any repair work begins, using acoustic listening equipment, thermal imaging, and pressure testing rather than opening up walls or flooring on a guess. That keeps the repair itself, whatever it turns out to be, limited to the actual problem area.

24/7 Emergency Response

When a Hidden Leak Is an Emergency

Not every leak needs a midnight call, but some do. Water coming through a ceiling, a meter that moves with every fixture off or a wet wall near wiring should be treated as an emergency. Our 24/7 line is open for these, and we send a technician with listening and pressure-testing equipment to find the source.

The goal is to locate the leak precisely so repairs stay small. We mark the spot, explain what we found and give you the price for the repair before anything is opened. If water is actively running, we can talk you through shutting off the supply while we head over.

In Carson, CA

Finding Hidden Leaks in Carson Homes

Carson's mix of slab-on-grade construction and older raised-foundation homes means leaks show up differently depending on the property. A slab leak often presents as a warm or damp spot on the floor, while a leak in a raised-foundation home's crawl space may go unnoticed longer since it's out of sight, sometimes only caught by a rising water bill or a musty smell developing underneath the house.

Acoustic leak detection equipment listens for the specific sound water makes escaping a pressurized line, which lets us pinpoint a leak's location within a small area rather than opening up a large section of slab or wall to search for it. For underground supply line leaks between the meter and the house, pressure testing isolates whether the leak is inside the home's plumbing or in the service line itself, which affects who's responsible for the repair in some cases.

Once we've located the leak, we'll walk through the repair options, sometimes a slab leak can be rerouted through an accessible wall or ceiling path instead of breaking through the concrete, which is often faster and less disruptive than the alternative.

A jump in the water bill is often the first sign of a hidden leak, and that applies equally to homes near Del Amo Park and the streets around the CSU Dominguez Hills campus. We use acoustic and pressure testing so only the exact leak location is opened.

Meter Test

Your Water Meter Is Running With Everything Off

A meter that keeps moving when every tap, appliance and sprinkler is off is one of the clearest signs of a leak. Cal Water, which serves most of Carson, describes the check this way: turn off all water inside and outside, then watch the test hand on the meter. If the test hand moves, water is passing through the meter.

A still test hand does not clear you completely, since some leaks only run when the irrigation system or a hot tub is on. A high bill with a quiet meter often traces back to irrigation valves, a pool fill line or a toilet that leaks now and then. We follow the same logic on site, then use listening and pressure tests to pinpoint the exact spot.

Inside the Structure

Leaks in Ceilings, Walls and Crawlspaces

Water staining on a ceiling often comes from a bathroom or supply line above, not from the spot where it shows. Leaks inside walls can run for weeks before bubbling paint or a musty smell gives them away, and crawlspace leaks show up as damp soil or a rising bill.

We trace the water back to its source before any drywall is opened, so the repair stays as small as it can. If drywall or flooring has already taken on water, our water damage restoration page explains what comes next.

Pricing Transparency

What Affects the Price of Leak Detection

We can't give an accurate number without seeing the job, but these are the real factors that move the price up or down.

Suspected leak location

A leak behind an accessible wall costs less to pinpoint than one suspected under a slab or underground.

How accessible the area is

Furniture, flooring, or landscaping over the suspected area can add time to reach and test it properly.

Whether damage has already appeared

A leak caught early, before any visible damage, is simpler to diagnose than one where water has already spread.

On insurance: The diagnostic visit itself is rarely something insurance covers, though if the leak turns out to be a sudden failure, the resulting repair or damage may be treated differently by your policy.
