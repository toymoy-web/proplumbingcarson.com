About Avalon Village

Avalon Village in South Carson

Avalon Village is a small residential area in the southern part of Carson. Mapping sites list it as a populated place within the city, near the crossing of Avalon Boulevard and Sepulveda Boulevard, and city-data shows it covering a little over half a square mile in ZIP 90745. We did not find an official city boundary, so treat its edges as approximate.

Streets listed in the area include East 230th Street, East 233rd Street, Wilmington Avenue and Banning Boulevard. Many of the small residential streets carry western names such as Boothill Lane and Lariat Lane. Mapping data puts the center of Carson about two miles to the northwest and Wilmington about two miles to the south.

Census-based estimates on city-data count about 930 homes. The largest group, around 390, were built in the 1950s, but there are also homes from the 1990s and the 2010s. Listing sites show small three-bedroom houses of about 1,100 square feet in planned developments with monthly HOA fees. A single street here can therefore mix older houses and newer HOA-managed homes.

Practical Guidance

Plumbing Where Older and Newer Homes Share the Same Streets

Because one street can hold homes from several decades, the plumbing is not uniform. Two neighbors can have different pipe materials and different water heaters. We identify what is installed in your home on site, which is why a short description of your home and its age helps when you call.

In a smaller home, a failed water heater or a supply leak can reach living space quickly because there is less room between the fixture and the floor. Know where your main water shutoff is. Closing it, or the cold inlet valve on the heater, limits the damage while you call for water heater repair or leak detection.

If your home is in an HOA-managed development, ask your management company whether the water meter, main shutoff or exterior lines belong to the association before you call a plumber. That answer decides who pays for what.
