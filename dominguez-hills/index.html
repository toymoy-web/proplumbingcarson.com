About Dominguez Hills

Where Dominguez Hills Is and Where the Name Comes From

The name comes from Dominguez Hill, where oil derricks stood by 1941, and from the Dominguez family, who received the Rancho San Pedro land grant in 1784 that Carson grew out of. California State University, Dominguez Hills was established in the early 1960s, and the campus gives the area its modern identity. The Dignity Health Sports Park, home of the LA Galaxy, is nearby at 18400 Avalon Boulevard.

Carson almost had a different name. Residents petitioned the county in June 1960 to incorporate a city called Dominguez. The county approved incorporation in 1967, and voters made it official in February 1968 as the city of Carson. Dominguez Hills is therefore a neighborhood and campus area inside Carson, not a separate city.

Housing in the area includes Dominguez Hills Village, which its association attorneys describe as a private, gated community of single-family homes with a clubhouse, pool and tennis courts. Listing sites say the homes were built between 1999 and 2003 and count fewer than 200 units. Different sites place it in ZIP 90745 or 90746, so use the ZIP printed on your own bill.

Practical Guidance

Plumbing in an HOA Community and Around the Campus

In a gated HOA community, find out which pipes are your responsibility. Associations often maintain shared lines and common areas, while the lines inside your home and your water heater are usually yours. Your governing documents settle the question, and the management office can tell you whether a plumber must give notice or be added to the gate list.

Newer construction still needs attention. A leak behind a wall can soak drywall and flooring before it shows, and leak detection finds the source without opening up more wall than necessary. Water heaters wear out on their own schedule whatever the age of the house, so a slow drop in hot water is worth a water heater repair call before it becomes a leak.

If you are replacing a fixture or a heater, ask your association whether it requires approval for exterior or visible changes. Permits for plumbing work are handled by the City of Carson Building and Safety Division.
