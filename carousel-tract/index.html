About Carousel Tract

Where the Carousel Tract Sits in Carson

The Carousel Tract is a residential pocket of Carson that residents, the city and the state all call by the same name. A CBS Los Angeles report on the 2013 city council action places it north of Lomita Boulevard between Avalon Boulevard and Main Street. We did not find published street boundaries, so if you are unsure whether your address is inside it, give us your street when you call.

The Los Angeles Regional Water Quality Control Board describes the tract as 285 single-family homes built in the 1960s, while the CBS report counts 278 homes. Either way, it is a compact neighborhood of detached houses. It has its own homeowners association, which is a useful first call for neighborhood notices.

The tract is also known beyond Carson because the state ordered Shell Oil in 2011 to investigate and clean up oil waste from a former tank farm beneath it. That is an environmental matter led by the Water Board, and we do not test soil or groundwater. For questions about the cleanup, contact the Water Board or the City of Carson. For a plumbing problem, call us.

Practical Guidance

Plumbing Guidance for a 1960s Single-Family Tract

A tract built in the 1960s is now more than sixty years old, but age alone does not tell you what is inside your walls. Some homes have had lines replaced and others have not. The reliable way to know is to look. A camera inspection of the sewer line and a pressure check on the supply side show what is actually installed, so decisions rest on evidence.

A damp patch in the yard, a water bill that climbed with no change in use, or a floor that feels warm are all reasons to book leak detection. For a slow drain that keeps coming back, ask for a camera look before paying for repeated clearing. If a water heater is leaking from the tank itself, replacement is usually the answer, and water heater repair covers the problems short of that.

Remodels and replacements in Carson can need a permit. Call the City of Carson Building and Safety Division to confirm what applies to your job before work starts.
